01 Your contact details

Renewing your motor insurance? Request a quotation.

Share your vehicle and insurance preferences. Our team can refer your request to an appropriate authorised insurance intermediary or provider, subject to your consent. Actual premiums and coverage depend on underwriting and insurer terms.

Motor insurance quotation enquiryAbout 3–5 minutes
01 Your contact details
02 Vehicle and policy information
03 Driving history and cover preferences

Privacy: Do not submit NRIC/FIN numbers, identification images, Singpass details, OTPs, full driving licences or policy documents through this initial enquiry. An authorised provider may request additional information securely to issue an accurate quote.

This form currently prepares a WhatsApp enquiry only. It does not automatically submit into CLM or request a live insurer premium. Shinytomato facilitates introductions; quotations and insurance advice are provided by appropriately authorised providers.

How it works
  1. Share your details.Provide vehicle, renewal and coverage preferences.
  2. Shinytomato reviews your request.We check whether additional details are needed.
  3. Relevant providers assess your enquiry.Authorised partners may provide available coverage and premium quotations.

Before you proceed

Quotes depend on the insurer's underwriting criteria, declared NCD, claims and driver details. No lowest-premium or acceptance guarantee is made.

Frequently asked questions

Does Shinytomato provide all these services directly?+

No. Our core work is automotive surface protection and detailing. Services on this page may be handled by independent third-party providers. We help with introductions or enquiries where suitable.

Can you guarantee the highest selling price or lowest financing rate?+

No. Car valuations, loan terms, insurance premiums and service quotations depend on individual circumstances and the relevant provider. You should compare offers and review all terms before proceeding.

Is there a fee to make an enquiry?+

There is no fee to send Shinytomato an initial WhatsApp enquiry. Any service fees, referral arrangements or commissions, if applicable, should be disclosed before you commit to a provider.

Will you share my contact details with a partner?+

Only with your permission for the relevant enquiry. Please do not send NRIC, banking passwords or sensitive financial documents through the initial WhatsApp message.
