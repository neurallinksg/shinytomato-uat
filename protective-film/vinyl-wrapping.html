Vinyl wrap care guide

Keep your new finish looking its best.

Follow the care instructions provided for your specific film. The guidance below is general and may be superseded by the material manufacturer’s recommendations.

Allow the wrap to settle

Avoid washing for at least 7 days after installation unless your installer advises otherwise. Report lifting edges or bubbles promptly.

Gentle hand washing

Use a pH-neutral automotive shampoo, clean soft mitt and microfibre drying towel. Rinse away grit first.

Avoid harsh chemicals

Do not use abrasive polishes, solvents, strong degreasers or automatic brush washes. Matte and satin films require finish-specific products.

Be careful with pressure washers

Avoid directing high-pressure water close to film edges, seams, badges or corners; keep the nozzle at a safe distance and angle.

Remove contamination promptly

Clean bird droppings, tree sap, insects and other contaminants as soon as practical using wrap-safe products.

Reduce prolonged exposure

Whenever practical, park under shade. Heat, UV, pollution and poor maintenance can accelerate fading or adhesive deterioration.

Vinyl wrap is a styling film — not paint protection film.

Standard vinyl wrap may offer some incidental surface coverage, but it should not be represented as equivalent to dedicated PPF for stone-chip or impact protection.

Before you book

Terms & conditions

These proposed service terms are intended to be confirmed in your written quotation and acceptance form. Applicable Singapore consumer law and any express written warranty take precedence.

1. Quotation, coverage and deposits

Pricing depends on vehicle dimensions, film selection, coverage, condition, disassembly needs and installation complexity. Quotes identify included and excluded areas. A booking deposit, if requested, is governed by the written quotation; cancellation or rescheduling terms must be disclosed before payment.

2. Existing paint, repairs and substrate condition

Wrapping requires clean, stable and suitably cured surfaces. Repainted, damaged, peeling, corroded or previously repaired panels may have poor adhesion or suffer paint lifting during installation or eventual removal. Pre-existing defects may remain visible. We may decline unsuitable areas or recommend repairs first.

3. Coverage limitations and finish appearance

Some deep recesses, tight edges, rubber seals, textured plastics, heavily curved pieces, hidden returns and high-heat areas may not be suitable. Edges, seams, joins, relief cuts and minor texture variations can be visible on close inspection. Exact panel boundaries are agreed before work begins.

4. Trims, emblems and dismantling

Some jobs require trim or emblem removal where appropriate. Aged clips, brittle plastics, previously repaired parts or adhesive-mounted badges may be fragile. Any additional removal, replacement or specialist repair work will be discussed and authorised before proceeding.

5. Colour, finish and sample variations

Screen colours and photographs are indicative. Actual colour, gloss, texture and metallic effect vary with lighting, film batches and panel geometry. Customers should approve a physical sample where practical. Exact future batch matches are not guaranteed.

6. Warranty and workmanship concerns

Film manufacturer warranty, if any, depends on the selected product and its written terms; no blanket warranty period is promised on this page. Report suspected workmanship issues promptly with photographs so they can be assessed. Defects caused by misuse, collisions, external damage, unsuitable cleaning, unauthorised modifications or normal wear are not covered unless expressly required by law.

7. Maintenance and expected service life

Service life varies by film, finish, environment, exposure and care. Follow film-specific instructions and return promptly for assessment if lifting, cracking or discolouration appears. High-contact areas, horizontal panels and exposed trims may wear faster.

8. Removal and future paintwork

Vinyl wrapping is not permanent, but safe or damage-free removal cannot be guaranteed, especially on non-original, weakened or repainted surfaces. Removal, adhesive cleaning, rewrapping and paint correction are separate services unless expressly included.

9. Road regulations and vehicle identification

Customers are responsible for ensuring colour changes and modifications comply with applicable Singapore requirements, including any required LTA or insurance notifications. We do not wrap or obscure legally required plates, lamps, safety sensors or markings.

10. Collection and acceptance

Customers should inspect the vehicle at handover and raise visible concerns. This does not remove statutory rights or prevent assessment of defects discovered later. Any agreed remedial work will be documented.
