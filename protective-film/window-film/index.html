OUR SPECIALIST DIFFERENCE

Factory tint? We work with the glass you've got.

Some cars already arrive with factory-tinted or privacy glass. Applying the same dark film to every window may breach Singapore's visibility rules. We assess your original glass before recommending a front/rear hybrid film plan.

1 · Measure2 · Match3 · Verify
LTA VLT requirements: Front windscreen and front side windows must transmit at least 70% visible light. Rear windscreen and rear side windows must transmit at least 25%. Film must also satisfy non-reflectivity and relevant electronic-system requirements. A film's VLT rating alone is not proof of road compliance.

If a front window cannot legally accept added film, we may leave it untreated. Hybrid film selections and supplementary UCX upgrades may affect the final quotation.

GOOD QUESTIONS. CLEAR ANSWERS.

Before you book.

How much does car window tint cost in Singapore?

Enquire for a personalised quotation for Shinytomato TERMINAX Ceramic-X Spectra, Ceramic-X IR and UltraCrystal-X packages. Vehicle dimensions, glazing and hybrid film requirements affect the final quotation.

Which tint offers the best heat rejection?

The highest published TSER values are achieved on darker shades, which may not suit legal road use on all windows. Choose by the actual shade-specific TSER, measured existing glass and your comfort needs—not by a single IR headline.

My car already has factory privacy glass. Can you tint it further?

Sometimes. We first measure the existing VLT, then evaluate suitable options for each pane. Some front or rear windows must remain untreated to maintain the required light transmission.

Is UCX 95 always LTA compliant?

No. UCX 95 has a nominal 90% VLT, but adding it to glass reduces the combined light transmission. Compliance depends on the final measured glass-and-film system and other LTA conditions.

What is the warranty for Terminax UCX?

Warranty coverage depends on the selected Terminax film and the written warranty issued with your installation. Ask our team to confirm the applicable term, qualifying defects and claim process for your chosen film before booking.

Will the small water bubbles disappear after installation?

Some temporary moisture haze or small water pockets can occur during curing. Drying depends on weather, film and glass. Dust, contamination, significant lifting or persistent defects should be inspected by the installer rather than assumed normal.

Can you remove my existing window film?

Yes, subject to glass condition and assessment. Existing aftermarket-film removal is separately quoted; factory-tinted glass itself is not a removable film. Rear demister lines and aged films can make removal higher risk.

BUY WITH CLARITY

Warranty & service terms.

Clear service information, installation limitations and warranty conditions should be easy to understand before you confirm your booking.

01 / Warranty coverage and limitations
  • Warranty coverage and duration depend on the selected TERMINAX film and the written warranty issued with the installation.
  • Where applicable, qualifying manufacturing defects may include eligible bubbling, peeling, delamination or abnormal discolouration attributable to the film, subject to the issued warranty terms.
  • Warranty does not cover accidental damage, scratches, abrasive cleaning, unauthorised removal or reinstallation, pre-existing glass defects, improper maintenance or normal wear that is not a covered manufacturing defect.
  • Window film does not guarantee a fixed cabin temperature, zero haze, immunity from scratches or legal compliance on every vehicle. Performance and suitability depend on the selected shade and the vehicle glass.
  • Our team will confirm the applicable film, coverage, warranty term and aftercare requirements in the final quotation or warranty documentation.
02 / Pricing, scope, deposits and booking
  • Prices are starting prices in SGD for eligible smaller vehicles and standard agreed glass coverage; vehicle size, additional windows, panoramic roof, removal, unusual glass, complex installation and hybrid mixing may change the quotation.
  • Standard quoted packages exclude sunroof and panoramic-roof film unless the written quotation explicitly includes it. Roof add-ons are subject to thermal stress checks and vehicle-manufacturer guidance, and may be declined if unsuitable.
  • Removal of existing aftermarket window film is a separate charge where required; factory privacy glass is not a removable aftermarket film.
  • Price inclusivity of GST, deposit amount and validity period must be clearly stated in the final written quotation before payment. Your booking is confirmed only after the agreed quotation and booking requirements are accepted.
  • Vehicle-specific film model, shade, window coverage, warranty and any surcharges must be agreed before commencement. If legal or technical constraints are found during inspection, revised options will be discussed before additional chargeable work.
  • Appointment timing is indicative and may change due to vehicle condition, installation complexity, supplier availability or safety concerns. Any cancellation, rescheduling or deposit treatment will follow the agreed booking terms and applicable law.
03 / Installation condition, curing and aftercare
  • Existing film, hard-water deposits, glass scratches, seals and rear-demister condition should be disclosed and assessed. Removal of aged film can carry risk, particularly on rear defroster lines or previously repaired glass.
  • Sunroof and panoramic-roof applications, when offered, may have product- and glass-specific restrictions and warranty exclusions. These will be stated on the final invoice and warranty document.
  • Newly applied window film may show temporary installation moisture or mild haze while curing. Follow the installer’s written guidance on window operation and cleaning; persistent defects should be reported for inspection.
  • Film installation is on the interior surface of automotive glass unless a specific product requires otherwise. Film cannot repair existing glass damage.
04 / LTA compliance and measurement limitations
  • Singapore front windscreen and front side windows require at least 70% VLT; rear windscreen and rear side windows require at least 25% VLT in the completed glazing system.
  • Non-reflectivity and applicable electronic-device communication rules also apply. Final compliance is determined by applicable regulatory requirements and measured installed glass, not shade names or online estimates.
  • Factory glass may restrict the use of additional film. Shinytomato may recommend changing the shade, mixing approved films, leaving glass untreated or not proceeding if a compliant outcome cannot be verified.
05 / Returns, changes, privacy and disputes
  • Installed film is a customised service; requests to change shade after installation may require removal and a new chargeable installation unless a legal or warranty remedy applies.
  • Customer enquiry details submitted through WhatsApp are used to respond to quotation and appointment requests. Avoid sending NRIC or sensitive personal data in the enquiry.
  • Any complaints should first be raised with Shinytomato together with the invoice, installation date, vehicle details and images of the reported issue. Statutory consumer rights are preserved.
  • Promotional offers, if any, are subject to their own expressly published conditions and must not override applicable law.

Final pricing, GST treatment, coverage and warranty terms will be stated in the written quotation or service documentation provided for your vehicle.
