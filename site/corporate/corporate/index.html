FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions from potential partners

Can Shinytomato be our outsourced service contractor?

We welcome outsourced service discussions for PPF, tint, coating, wraps and related surface work. Suitability depends on scheduling, vehicle volumes and the agreed responsibilities of each party.

Do you support both new and used vehicle dealers?

Yes. We can discuss pre-delivery presentation, paint correction, protection packages and handover preparation suited to the condition of each vehicle.

Can you provide recurring fleet or leasing support?

Yes, subject to an agreed programme, capacity and commercial terms. Tell us your vehicle count, typical services and required turnaround so we can evaluate an appropriate approach.

Can your business handle warranty and after-sales follow-ups?

We can discuss inspection and after-sales coordination for services we perform. Product warranty eligibility and workmanship support are governed by the applicable terms and agreed responsibilities.

Do you offer referral, dealer or contracted pricing?

Commercial proposals are prepared individually according to service scope, volume, operational requirements and expected support. We do not publish blanket B2B pricing on this page.
