What this inspection means for you

01 · Record

Identify the vehicle and record its exterior condition with appropriate photographs.

02 · Assess

Inspect paintwork, panels, glass, trims and existing film or repair risks.

03 · Explain

Discuss pre-existing damage, exclusions, extra preparation and any concerns before installation.

04 · Document

Keep a clear checklist to support workmanship, quality control and customer handover.

Designed for careful workmanship

A strong finish begins with knowing the vehicle’s starting condition. Our proposed inspection framework is intended to make pre-existing chips, scratches, repainted areas, loose trims, damaged glass and other risks easier to identify and discuss—not to imply the vehicle is defect-free.

Inspection is visual and non-invasive. It is not a mechanical safety inspection, paint-thickness certification or guarantee that hidden defects will be detected.

Official product partner identity

The checklist can support PPF, window film, coating and vinyl projects. Service-specific points that do not apply should be marked N/A rather than assumed completed.
