Why Sunroof Heat Shield Matters

Sunroof heat is different, and normal tint is not always enough.

Overhead glass brings a very direct kind of heat into the vehicle. Even when the air-conditioning feels cold, the top of the cabin can still feel hot because of the sunroof area.

That is why many owners of panoramic-roof vehicles look for a more dedicated heat shield solution instead of relying only on standard side-window tint performance.

At Shinytomato, we position sunroof heat shield installation as a targeted comfort upgrade for vehicles that need stronger overhead solar control.

Terminax Sunroof-X

Built for sunroof use, not just general darkening.

Terminax describes Sunroof-X Shade Film as a Ceramic-X composite sunroof protection film for heat insulation and UV blocking.

It is designed to preserve visibility while improving heat control and is stated to suit panoramic, sliding and fixed sunroof types.

That makes it a more focused solution for overhead glass areas where comfort is usually the main concern.
