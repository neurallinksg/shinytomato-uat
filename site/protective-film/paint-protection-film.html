Frequently asked questions

Know before you protect.

Is PPF the same as ceramic coating?

No. PPF is a physical protective film designed to help resist certain impacts and abrasions. Ceramic coating is a liquid-applied protective surface treatment, not a substitute for impact protection.

Can PPF prevent all scratches and stone chips?

No. PPF can reduce certain kinds of damage but it cannot prevent every impact, scratch or puncture.

Can I change my car colour with PPF?

Yes, colour PPF ranges offer visual transformation with protective film characteristics. Available finishes and colours vary by product.

Will existing PPF or vinyl wrapping affect my quotation?

Yes. Standard installation quotations assume suitable, exposed paintwork unless otherwise stated. Existing PPF or vinyl wrap may need to be removed. Film removal, adhesive residue cleaning, paint inspection, correction and other preparation may involve additional labour and cost. We will assess the vehicle and obtain your agreement to any additional charges before proceeding. Removal may expose or damage weak, repainted or previously repaired paintwork.

Repainted surfaces need individual assessment. Film adhesion or removal can carry additional risks if underlying paintwork is compromised.

Warranty & terms

  • Warranty eligibility requires qualifying installation and proof of purchase; keep your invoice and film identification details.
  • Manufacturer-covered defects may include qualifying cracking, yellowing, peeling, delamination, discolouration, adhesive failure and bubbling.
  • Claims require inspection and approval under the manufacturer's applicable written terms.
  • Accidental damage, normal physical wear, misuse and improper maintenance are not automatically covered.

Maintenance requirements

  • Allow the film to cure. TERMINAX's published guidance varies: at least one week on its e-warranty page, and 2–4 weeks in its general care policy. Follow the specific written handover instructions; do not wash until cleared.
  • Hand-wash using mild / pH-neutral shampoo and a clean, soft wash mitt; dry gently with microfibre.
  • Avoid abrasive brushes, harsh chemicals, solvents and direct high-pressure spraying at film edges.
  • Remove bird droppings, bug residue, tree sap and other contamination promptly. Inspect edges regularly; periodic professional care is recommended.

Limitations to understand

  • PPF reduces certain stone chips and surface marks but is not scratch-proof, impact-proof or puncture-proof.
  • Self-healing is generally limited to minor surface-level marks; deep cuts and torn film will not heal.
  • Edges, seams, curves and complex panels may remain visible; coverage depends on the agreed installation scope.
  • Previously repainted, weak or damaged paint can lift during installation or removal. Assessment and written acknowledgement may be needed.

Detailed customer terms & care guidance

01 — Warranty eligibility, documentation & claims

Manufacturer warranty is conditional, not an unconditional promise of replacement. Eligibility can depend on the installed product, authorised/certified installation, invoice, film/roll identification, registration and correct care. Contact Shinytomato promptly with photographs and your invoice if you suspect a defect. The installer and/or manufacturer may need to inspect the film before determining the remedy. Written manufacturer terms govern any approved claim; the manufacturer warranty does not automatically cover the cost of unrelated repairs, repainting or vehicle downtime.

02 — Post-installation curing & washing

Adhesive curing is affected by temperature, humidity, panel shape and film type. Do not wash or use high-pressure water during the initial curing period specified at handover. TERMINAX publishes differing general waiting periods (at least 7 days versus 2–4 weeks); we will confirm the applicable instruction for your job in writing. Some temporary moisture haze or small water pockets can settle during curing; do not pierce, press or peel the film yourself. Report persistent issues for inspection.

03 — Safe cleaning and periodic care

Use a gentle pre-rinse, suitable pH-neutral shampoo, clean wash media and microfibre drying towels. Avoid brush-type automatic washes, polishing compounds, abrasive pads and aggressive chemicals unless the manufacturer and installer approve a specific product. Never direct a pressure-washer jet at film edges or seams. Remove acidic and staining contaminants promptly. TERMINAX recommends routine PPF care approximately quarterly; ask us about a suitable paid maintenance service.

04 — Physical damage, environmental exposure & appearance

High-energy impacts, sharp objects, vandalism, collision damage, strong chemical exposure, persistent stains and improper washing can damage PPF. Self-healing cannot repair deep cuts, punctures, film tears or damaged paint underneath. Colour and sheen can vary with lighting, panel geometry, batch and the original paint. Film joins, relief cuts and edges may be visible at close range. These practical characteristics are not automatically manufacturing defects.

05 — Existing PPF / vinyl wrap: removal and pricing

Unless specifically included in your written quotation, prices exclude removal of existing PPF, colour PPF, vinyl wrap, decals and adhesive residue, and any additional decontamination, polishing, paint correction or panel preparation. Costs depend on the area covered, age and condition of existing film, adhesive bonding, access and underlying paint condition. A preliminary estimate may change if hidden conditions are discovered during removal; any revised charges will be explained for your approval before additional work proceeds. Film removal may lift or damage weak paint, clearcoat, resprayed or repaired surfaces. Shinytomato will discuss foreseeable risks and assess suitable next steps; no removal outcome can be guaranteed.

06 — Repainted surfaces, removal and panel repairs

Factory paint and previously refinished surfaces carry different risks. Film may pull away weak paint or clearcoat during removal, particularly on repaired, resprayed or poorly bonded panels. Please disclose prior repainting, bodywork and paint defects before booking. Removal and reinstallation after collision or body repairs are separate services unless specifically included in a written quotation or an approved claim.

07 — Marine-X applications

Marine-X is intended for selected marine surfaces. Suitability depends on the substrate, exposure, immersion conditions, contours and access for installation. It is not a substitute for antifouling systems, structural repair or corrosion control. Marine installation scope, aftercare and any locally available written warranty must be agreed separately before booking.
