Before you book

Terms, eligibility & aftercare.

Which programmes qualify?

The 5-Year Car Care Program is offered only with specified eligible TERMINAX flagship PPF installations as confirmed in the final invoice or warranty documents. The S$1,288 hybrid offer uses Clear Pro Clear-Plus PPF and ceramic coating; it does not automatically include the TERMINAX flagship care programme. Launch discount applies only to explicitly approved TERMINAX packages.

Do promotions combine?

No. Promotional prices, discounts, service credits, freebies, sales consultant incentives and referral benefits are non-stackable unless Shinytomato expressly approves a written exception. Each promotional benefit is non-transferable and cannot be exchanged for cash or other products or services.

Launch period, booking and 50% deposit

The advertised New Studio Launch privilege runs 26 November 2026 through 27 January 2027, subject to stock, appointment capacity and a qualifying quotation. A 50% deposit is requested to confirm an accepted reservation. Payment and cancellation conditions must be supplied before the deposit is collected. An enquiry does not guarantee eligibility or availability.

Car Care credit and session rules

The S$1,000 maintenance-care benefit is allocated at S$200 per programme year for up to five years. Outdoor plan: quarterly care with a maximum S$50 offset per eligible session; indoor plan: half-yearly care with a maximum S$100 offset per eligible session. Benefits reduce the invoiced price of eligible maintenance services; customers pay any remaining balance. Unused benefits are not redeemable for cash. Detailed expiry, rollover, redemption and transfer conditions should be confirmed in the signed programme document.

Film, coating and workmanship warranties

Manufacturer warranties, installer workmanship support and ceramic-coating warranties are distinct and depend on the selected product, customer maintenance, vehicle condition and written warranty terms. No film or coating prevents all stone chips, damage, scratches, stains or water spots. Existing resprayed, repaired or damaged paint may carry elevated removal or installation risks.

Care instructions after installation

Follow the installer-specific curing and first-wash instructions given during handover. Thereafter use pH-appropriate wash products, a soft mitt and clean microfiber towels; rinse off bird droppings, sap and insect residue promptly; do not direct high-pressure water closely at exposed film edges; avoid abrasive polishes, harsh solvents and aggressive brushing. Return promptly if edges lift, bubbling persists or film is damaged.

GST, inspection and pricing variations

Displayed S$1,288 package and S$600 upgrade-from amounts are before GST. The 20% launch privilege is calculated on the eligible regular pre-GST price, with GST added separately. Final cost depends on vehicle condition, coverage, preparation, panel complexity and authorised extras. The written quotation controls.

Statutory consumer rights

Nothing in these terms excludes or restricts non-excludable rights under applicable Singapore law. The final accepted quotation and programme documents should be reviewed and approved by Shinytomato before publication and booking acceptance.

Website content is a draft of customer-facing offer conditions and requires business approval. Film coverage, dates and benefits follow the supplied promotional artwork; where campaign terms differ, the signed quotation and programme terms must resolve the differences.
