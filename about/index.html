Our Story

A professional studio with a humble approach

ShinyTomato has spent years serving vehicle owners who value finish quality, protection and careful workmanship. Over time, our studio has developed through hands-on experience, continuous learning and a commitment to doing the work properly.

We are proud of what we do, but we also believe professionalism should be shown through standards and results rather than noise. That is why we continue to invest in our knowledge, our processes and the products we use.

Whether the vehicle is a daily-driven family car, an executive sedan or an enthusiast-owned performance car, our purpose remains the same: to deliver premium detailing with integrity, discipline and respect for every vehicle entrusted to us.

Sustainable Detailing

Eco-conscious without compromising standards

ShinyTomato adopts a water-saving detailing approach that significantly reduces water usage compared with conventional washing methods. Our eco-conscious system allows us to operate in a cleaner and more controlled environment while maintaining the premium finish quality our customers expect.
