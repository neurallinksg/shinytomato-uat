What Is Eco Groom

More structured care, built for real vehicle conditions.

Eco Groom is Shinytomato’s focused grooming line designed around specific problems instead of using one generic wash-and-clean package for every car.

Each service is purpose-built to resolve a defined issue — whether that means pest treatment, anti-bacterial cabin recovery, pet fur removal, serious interior restoration, scratch improvement, headlight clarity, water mark removal or contamination maintenance for protected surfaces.

Specialised detailing is not the same as ordinary car grooming. It requires trained judgement, proper tool selection, suitable chemical control, surface awareness and skilled technicians who understand how to solve a problem professionally instead of simply making the car look cleaner for a short while.

Why Skilled Work Matters

Correct treatment, correct technique and correct hands.

Many vehicle issues cannot be solved properly with common detailing methods or shortcut cleaning.

Delicate interior materials, coated surfaces, wrapped finishes, PPF films, scratched paint and contaminated glass all require the right treatment approach.

That is why Shinytomato positions Eco Groom as a more specialised detailing service carried out by technicians with practical experience, not just routine washing staff.
