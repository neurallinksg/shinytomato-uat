Frequently asked questions

Before you book

Which package suits my car?

Deluxe focuses on everyday ceramic protection, Prime adds a CSL-based coating, Titanium extends protection across more exterior surfaces, and Flagship uses the Crystal Serum Ultra system. Share your car model and parking conditions for a recommendation.

Does ceramic coating remove scratches?

No. Paint correction is part of the advanced polishing process under Eco-Posh Standard and above. It is not included in wax polishing or maintenance polishing. Ceramic coating itself does not remove scratches or repair damaged paint.

Is paint correction included?

Paint correction is part of the advanced polishing process under Eco-Posh Standard and above. It is not included in wax polishing or maintenance polishing. Your quotation will confirm the polishing approach for your vehicle.

How do I maintain my coating?

Wash regularly with appropriate car-care products and avoid harsh cleaning methods. We will explain the care requirements for your selected coating.

Is ceramic coating the same as paint protection film?

No. Ceramic coatings primarily help with surface maintenance and environmental resistance; PPF is a physical film designed to help protect against stone chips and certain abrasion.
